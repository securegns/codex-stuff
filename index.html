The Vespera method

From ambiguity to a defensible decision.

Good advisory work is not more paperwork. It is a disciplined sequence that reduces uncertainty and makes ownership visible.

01

Frame the matter

Define the decision, business objective, constraints, stakeholders and deadlines before solving the wrong problem.

02

Map the exposure

Separate contractual, operational, regulatory and jurisdictional risk — including what is known, assumed and missing.

03

Build options

Compare realistic paths, dependencies and trade-offs in language leadership teams can act on.

04

Coordinate execution

Translate the decision into owners, documents, specialist counsel needs and concrete next actions.
